Failure in a dream

You fail.

The dream warns of mistakes in your affairs: fix them before they turn into real losses.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • If someone in love dreams of being turned down, it means the opposite: they are already loved and just need to be bolder.
  • For a young woman, the dream advises making better use of her opportunities.
