A horseshoe in a dream

You see a horseshoe.

Your affairs will move forward, and love will bring luck.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • A broken horseshoe: bad luck, so look after your health.
  • A horseshoe hanging on a fence: success beyond your hopes.
  • Picking one up on the road: profit from an unexpected source.
