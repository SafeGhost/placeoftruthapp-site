A lily in a dream

You see a lily.

Sadness and worry over loved ones' health may come, but the trial will make you wiser.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Lilies blooming among rich leaves mean an early marriage, but also partings.
  • For a young woman picking lilies, the dream promises joy mixed with sorrow.
  • Withered lilies mean sadness is closer than you think.
  • Breathing in the scent of lilies means sorrow will purify you and make you stronger.
