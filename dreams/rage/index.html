Rage in a dream

You are in a rage, shouting and smashing things.

Quarrels lie ahead, and your friends may get hurt.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Seeing others in a rage — poor conditions at work and discord in your social life.
  • Your beloved in a rage — a sour note and misunderstandings in your relationship.
