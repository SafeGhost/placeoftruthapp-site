Ivy in a dream

You see ivy on the wall of a house.

Good health, a rising income and plenty of joy are waiting for you.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • For a young woman, ivy promises honour and recognition.
  • Ivy on a wall in moonlight: secret dates.
  • Withered ivy: a broken engagement and sadness.
