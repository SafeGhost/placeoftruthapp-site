The sky in a dream

You see a clear sky.

Honors and interesting trips with pleasant company await you.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • A gloomy sky: your hopes may not come true.
  • Floating in the sky among strange faces and beasts: painful jealousy.
  • The sky turning red: unrest in society.
