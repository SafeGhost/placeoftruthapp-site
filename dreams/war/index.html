War in a dream

You dream of war.

Your affairs will run into difficulties, and there will be discord and turmoil at home.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • For a young woman, seeing her partner go to war means she will hear unpleasant news about him.
  • Your country losing a war points to upheaval in your affairs.
  • Dreaming of victory means busy work and harmony in the family.
