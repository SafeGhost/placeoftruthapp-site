A tree in a dream

You see trees in fresh leaves.

Your hopes and wishes will come true happily.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Dead trees mean sadness and loss.
  • Climbing a tree means a quick promotion.
  • Cutting down or uprooting a tree means wasting your energy and money.
