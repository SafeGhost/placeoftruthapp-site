A dead body in a dream

You see a corpse.

The dream foretells sad news from people far away and a downturn in your affairs.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • A body dressed in black — a tangled, difficult matter.
  • A battlefield covered in bodies — strife and general discontent.
  • A dead animal — stagnation at work and low energy.
