A fountain in a dream

You see a fountain sparkling in the sun.

A clear, sparkling fountain means wealth, great joy and pleasant travels.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • A muddy fountain: insincere people around you and setbacks in love.
  • A dry, broken fountain: the end of familiar pleasures.
