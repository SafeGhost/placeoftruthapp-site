A trap in a dream

You are caught in a trap.

Rivals may outwit you. Be more careful.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Setting a trap means you'll use cunning to get your way.
  • Catching game in a trap means success in whatever you do.
  • An empty trap means bad luck.
  • An old or broken trap means a slump at work.
