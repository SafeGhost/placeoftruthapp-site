A bridge in a dream

You see a long, rickety bridge vanishing into darkness.

Gloom and hard times ahead; for those in love, disappointment in the chosen one.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Crossing a bridge safely means you will overcome difficulties, even if your means seem shaky.
  • A bridge collapsing before you warns of betrayal and false admirers.
  • Clear water below means prosperity; muddy water means wasted effort.
