Old age in a dream

You see yourself grown old.

Something you have started may fail; do not rush and check your plans.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Arguing about your age — your stubbornness will anger your family.
  • For a young woman, seeing her beloved grown old — look after the relationship; it can be lost through neglect.
