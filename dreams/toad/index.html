A toad in a dream

You see a toad.

This points to ill-fated adventures. Don't take needless risks.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • For a woman, it advises guarding her good name against gossip.
  • Killing a toad means your judgment will be harshly criticized.
  • Holding a toad means you may unwittingly let a friend down.
