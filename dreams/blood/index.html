Blood in a dream

You see blood.

Be careful: ill-wishers may try to block your success.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Bloodstained clothes warn you against dubious new friendships.
  • Blood flowing from a wound means tiredness and worry; save your strength.
  • Blood on your hands means bad luck unless you take care of yourself and your affairs.
  • Bleeding heavily means gossip about you.
