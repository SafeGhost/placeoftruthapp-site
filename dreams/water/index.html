Water in a dream

You see clear water.

Well-being and joy await you.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Muddy water warns of danger and gloom.
  • Water rising in your house means you will fight trouble and win if it goes down.
  • Falling into muddy water points to bitter mistakes.
  • Drinking clear water means your hopes will come true.
  • Splashing in water means love and passion are awakening.
