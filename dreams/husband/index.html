A husband in a dream

You see your husband cheerful and handsome.

Your home will fill with happiness, and bright prospects lie ahead.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Your husband leaves you without explanation: some bitterness, then an unexpected reconciliation.
  • Your husband looks pale and tired: look after the family's health.
  • He is in love with someone else: he is getting bored, so bring new life to your relationship.
  • An unmarried woman sees herself married: work on yourself before looking for a partner.
