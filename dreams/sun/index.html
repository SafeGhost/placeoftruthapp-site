The sun in a dream

You see a clear sunrise.

Joyful events and well-being await you.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • The sun at noon means your ambitions will come true.
  • A sunset means success has peaked, so protect what you have.
  • The sun through clouds means troubles are letting go.
  • An eclipse means a stormy time, after which things will be better than before.
