Beads in a dream

You see beads.

Influential people will take notice of you.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Counting beads — quiet joy and contentment.
  • Stringing beads — support from wealthy people.
  • Scattering beads — losing respect among your acquaintances.
