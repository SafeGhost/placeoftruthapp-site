The dead in a dream

You see someone who has died and talk with them.

This is a warning dream: be careful with your affairs and agreements.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • A late father warns against risky deals.
  • A late mother asks you to drive away gloom and grudges.
  • A late brother or friend means someone will soon ask for your help.
  • If the dead ask you for a promise, heed their advice.
