Legs in a dream

You see your own legs, strong and well shaped.

A happy future and devoted friends await you.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Admiring someone's beautiful legs warns you not to lose your head over a crush.
  • A wounded leg means losses.
  • A wooden leg warns you not to pretend in front of friends.
  • Three or more legs mean more plans than real gain.
  • Being unable to walk means tight money.
