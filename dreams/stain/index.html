A stain in a dream

You see a stain on your clothes.

Trouble over small things is coming. Don't blow it out of proportion.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • A stain on your hands means the same small worries.
  • A stain on someone else's clothes or skin means someone may let you down.
