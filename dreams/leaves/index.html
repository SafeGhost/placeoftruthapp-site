Leaves in a dream

You see green leaves.

Happiness and clear progress in your affairs await you.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Withered leaves mean false hopes and gloom.
  • For a young woman, fresh green leaves promise an inheritance and a good marriage, while dry ones mean a lonely stretch for a while.
