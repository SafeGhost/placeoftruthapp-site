An ox in a dream

You see a well-fed ox.

You will become a noticeable, respected person in your circle.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Fat oxen in a green pasture promise luck and a rise beyond your hopes.
  • Lean oxen mean your means may shrink and friends drift away.
  • Oxen yoked together promise a happy, comfortable marriage.
  • Oxen drinking from a clear stream mean a long-held dream will come true.
  • A dead ox foretells sadness.
