Figs in a dream

You see figs growing on a tree.

Figs on the branches point to good health and profit.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Eating figs: look after your health and don't overdo it.
  • For a young woman, figs growing on a tree promise a good marriage to a well-off person.
