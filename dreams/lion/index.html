A lion in a dream

You see a lion.

A great force is driving you forward.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Overpowering a lion means victory in any contest.
  • If the lion overpowers you, enemies may get the upper hand.
  • Caged lions mean success depends on handling opposition.
  • Lion cubs mean new ventures that will succeed with proper care.
  • Hearing a lion roar means an unexpected promotion.
  • Riding a lion means courage and persistence in hardship.
  • A lion snarling over you warns you to be careful on your way up.
