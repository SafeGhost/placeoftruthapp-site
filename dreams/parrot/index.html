A parrot in a dream

You hear parrots chattering.

There will be a lot of idle talk and gossip around you, so don't take it seriously.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Parrots sitting quietly promise a break in family arguments.
  • Teaching a parrot to talk means trouble in your private affairs.
  • For a young woman, owning a parrot warns that her partner may think her quarrelsome.
