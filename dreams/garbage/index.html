Garbage in a dream

You see heaps of garbage.

Beware of rumours and stay out of shady dealings.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Clearing or taking out the garbage: getting rid of what weighs you down.
  • For a woman, the dream advises not trusting those who speak badly of her.
