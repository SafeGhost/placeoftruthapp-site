A father in a dream

You see your father.

A tricky situation lies ahead, and wise advice will help you get out of it.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • If your late father appears, your affairs are heavy going and need careful handling.
  • For a young woman, it is a hint to check how sincere her partner is.
