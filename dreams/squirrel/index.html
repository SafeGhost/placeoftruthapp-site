A squirrel in a dream

You see a squirrel.

Pleasant friends will visit soon, and your affairs will go up.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Petting a squirrel means joy in the family.
  • Killing one means you'll be seen as unfriendly.
  • A dog chasing a squirrel means quarrels among friends.
