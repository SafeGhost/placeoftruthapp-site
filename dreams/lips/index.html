Lips in a dream

You see full, beautiful lips.

Harmony and comfort lie ahead, and in love, affection that is returned and faithful.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Thick, unattractive lips mean unpleasant encounters, hasty decisions and short tempers.
  • Thin lips mean you will master the most complex matters.
  • Sore or swollen lips mean hardship and unwise cravings.
