Bees in a dream

You see bees.

Pleasant and profitable work lies ahead: your efforts will bring joy and income.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • For a boss, bees promise a loyal team; for a trader, growing sales; for parents, joy from their children.
  • A bee sting means someone close may hurt or let you down.
