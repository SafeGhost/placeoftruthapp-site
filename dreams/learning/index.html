Learning in a dream

You are learning something new.

You will be drawn to knowledge, and if you use your time well you will go far.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Entering a university or school means rising from obscurity and earning more.
  • Seeing learned people means interesting, influential friends.
  • For a woman, it promises success for her ambitions.
