A hat in a dream

You put on a new hat.

A profitable change of place or job lies ahead.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • For a woman, a beautiful new hat means wealth and admiration.
  • Losing your hat: setbacks at work and broken appointments.
  • The wind blowing your hat off: sudden changes, not all of them welcome.
