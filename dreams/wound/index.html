A wound in a dream

You are wounded.

Distress and an unlucky turn in your affairs lie ahead.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Seeing others wounded means friends may treat you unfairly.
  • Dressing a wound means you will have reason to be glad of your good luck.
