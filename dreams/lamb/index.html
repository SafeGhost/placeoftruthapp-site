A lamb in a dream

You see lambs playing in a green meadow.

Pure friendships, joy and growing prosperity await you.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Lambs nursing from their mothers mean a happy home and lovely children.
  • Carrying a lamb in your arms means sweet cares for those you love.
  • A lost lamb means someone follows your example, so mind your actions.
  • Dogs or wolves attacking lambs mean innocent people will suffer from schemers.
  • Lambs in rain or a snowstorm mean hopes that fall short.
