A chrysanthemum in a dream

You pick chrysanthemums.

Coloured chrysanthemums promise pleasant meetings; white ones bring sadness and confusion.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • A bouquet of chrysanthemums — love will be offered to you; don't turn it down out of empty pride.
  • An avenue of white chrysanthemums — a quiet sorrow that will leave you wiser and stronger.
