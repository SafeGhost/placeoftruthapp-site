Paint in a dream

You see a freshly painted house.

The plan you have in mind will succeed.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Painting yourself means you are happy with your work.
  • Paint on your clothes means someone's careless criticism will upset you.
  • Beautiful paintings warn that not all that glitters is gold: some friends may be less sincere than they seem.
