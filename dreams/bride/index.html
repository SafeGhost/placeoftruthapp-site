A bride in a dream

You are a bride, happily dressing for your wedding.

A pleasant surprise that will delight you.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • If dressing feels joyless, your hopes may not come true.
  • Kissing a bride means friends will make up.
  • A bride kissing you means good health and unexpected luck for your sweetheart.
  • A bride looking tired and pale means success will not satisfy you.
