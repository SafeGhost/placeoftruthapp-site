A mole in a dream

You see a mole.

You have hidden ill-wishers, so keep an eye on the people around you.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Catching a mole means you will overcome resistance and rise.
  • Moles on the skin warn you to look after your health and avoid quarrels.
