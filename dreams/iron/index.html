Iron in a dream

You see iron.

A hard time is ahead. Be ready for worries and hold on.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • An iron weight pressing on you: anxiety and losses.
  • Rusty iron: shortage of money and disappointment.
  • Red-hot iron: your energy is going the wrong way, so aim it wisely.
  • Striking with iron: do not be harsh with those who depend on you.
