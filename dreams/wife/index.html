A wife in a dream

You dream of your wife.

Your affairs are still unsettled, and there may be disagreements at home.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • A wife who is especially warm in the dream means profit from an important venture.
  • For a wife, seeing her husband scold her points to criticism and confusion at home.
