Frost in a dream

You see frost on a dark, gloomy morning.

You may end up in a strange land, but your wanderings will end in peace.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Frost on a sunlit landscape: you'll turn away from empty pleasures and earn respect.
  • For a young woman, the dream advises caring for her partner's feelings while he is away.
