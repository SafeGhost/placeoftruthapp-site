A wallet in a dream

You find a wallet full of money.

You will be lucky: nearly all your wishes will come true.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • An empty wallet — disappointment in a big hope.
  • Losing your wallet — a falling-out with your best friend.
  • A purse full of jewels and new bills — you will be surrounded by cheerful, loving, harmonious people.
