Clouds in a dream

You see dark, heavy clouds.

Dark clouds warn of setbacks caused by poor decisions.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Rain falling from them — worries and tiredness.
  • Bright clouds with the sun shining through — success after hard times.
  • Clouds with stars — brief joys and small wins.
