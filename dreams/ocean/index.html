The ocean in a dream

You see a calm ocean.

Good days await you: work will bring income and love will bring joy.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Waves pounding a ship far out at sea warn of trouble at work and stormy days at home.
  • Watching foaming waves from the shore means a narrow escape from harm and from others' schemes.
  • An ocean so shallow you can see the bottom promises joy mixed with worries.
