A mountain in a dream

You climb a green mountain slope.

You will quickly gain wealth and standing.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • A steep mountain whose top you can't reach warns of setbacks, so work on your weak points.
  • Waking at a dangerous spot in the climb means things will take a sudden turn for the better.
  • Getting tired and stopping means your success will be a little smaller than you hoped.
