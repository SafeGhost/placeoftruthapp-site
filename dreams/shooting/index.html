Shooting in a dream

You hear gunshots.

Selfishness may bring discord to a couple, and carelessness may bring setbacks at work.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Being shot: friends will suddenly turn on you, but later you will fully make up.
  • For a young woman, seeing her partner with a gun: a serious falling-out.
