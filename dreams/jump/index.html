A jump in a dream

You jump over an obstacle.

Everything you take on will work out.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Jumping and falling back: annoying troubles, so be patient.
  • Jumping down from a wall: do not gamble money blindly and do not rush in love.
