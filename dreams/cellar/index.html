A cellar in a dream

You are in a cold, damp cellar.

Doubts and gloomy thoughts will weigh on you; gather your will to drive them off. Look after your property.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • A cellar stocked with wine and food means you will be offered a share of profits from a doubtful source; think before agreeing.
