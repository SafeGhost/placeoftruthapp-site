A ring in a dream

You are wearing a ring.

New ventures are ahead, and they will succeed.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • A broken ring: quarrels in marriage or a parting for lovers.
  • For a young woman, receiving a ring means her worries about her partner will end; he will be devoted to her.
  • Seeing rings on others: growing prosperity and new friends.
