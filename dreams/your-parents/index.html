Your parents in a dream

You see your parents cheerful and healthy.

There will be harmony and pleasant people in your life, and your work and love will thrive.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Your parents happy in your home promise pleasant changes.
  • Parents who have passed away appearing in a dream ask you to be more careful in your affairs.
  • Pale parents or parents dressed in black foretell disappointments.
  • Sad or unwell parents warn that you may miss good chances.
