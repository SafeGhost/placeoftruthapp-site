Fingers in a dream

You see beautiful clean fingers.

Your love will be returned, and your kindness will earn you a good name.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Dirty or scratched, bleeding fingers mean hardships and low spirits.
  • Fingers cut off warn that ill-wishers may take part of your income or inheritance.
