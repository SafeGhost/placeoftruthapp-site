Ice in a dream

You see ice.

Worry is ahead: ill-wishers will try to spoil your best work.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Walking on ice: do not risk what is solid for a passing thrill.
  • Icicles on the roof: a lack of comfort, so look after your health.
  • Icicles falling from trees: trouble will soon melt away.
  • Eating ice: look after your health.
