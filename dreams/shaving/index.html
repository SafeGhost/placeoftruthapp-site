Shaving in a dream

You are shaving.

You will run your own affairs and household, even if there are some arguments.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Someone else shaves you: do not let yourself be fooled.
  • A smooth face afterwards: calm days.
  • A dull razor pulling your skin: give people no reason to gossip.
  • Only thinking about a shave: good plans, but not enough drive.
