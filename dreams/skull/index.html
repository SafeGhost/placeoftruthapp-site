A skull in a dream

You see grinning skulls.

Quarrels at home and a slowdown at work are possible.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Seeing a friend's skull: someone will harm you out of envy.
  • Seeing your own skull: you will blame yourself for the past; let it go.
