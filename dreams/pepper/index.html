Pepper in a dream

Pepper burns your tongue.

Your love of gossip will cause trouble with people you know.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Red peppers growing — a thrifty, independent partner.
  • Heaps of pepper pods — you will firmly defend your rights.
  • Grinding black pepper — beware of clever schemers.
  • A pepper shaker on the table — sharp words and quarrels.
