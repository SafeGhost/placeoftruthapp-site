A train in a dream

You see a train racing at great speed.

A quick rise in fortune and travel abroad await you.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • A broken-down train means hitches at work and postponed trips.
  • A wrecked train means great distress and losses.
  • Hearing a train approach means news from far away.
  • A train whistle means a happy reunion with a long-absent friend or a profitable offer.
