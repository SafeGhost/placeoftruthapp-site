A donkey in a dream

You are riding a donkey.

Journeys to distant lands and hard but exciting paths lie ahead.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • A donkey braying in your face warns of public rudeness.
  • Falling off a donkey means setbacks and a lovers' quarrel.
  • Being given or buying a donkey promises success and a happy marriage.
  • A white donkey means lasting wealth and time for what you love.
