A kiss in a dream

You kiss your mother.

Success in your affairs, respect and love from friends are waiting for you.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Children kissing: happy family get-togethers.
  • Kissing your loved one in the light: your intentions are honest; in the dark: beware of dubious affairs.
  • A rival kisses your loved one: you risk losing their affection.
  • Kissing an enemy: making up with an offended friend.
