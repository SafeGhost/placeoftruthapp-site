Mud in a dream

You walk through mud.

A friend may let you down, and there may be trouble at home.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Seeing others in mud brings ugly rumors about someone you know.
  • Mud on your clothes means someone is attacking your good name.
  • Scraping it off means you will clear yourself of slander.
