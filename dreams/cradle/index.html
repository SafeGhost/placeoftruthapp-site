A cradle in a dream

You see a cradle with a beautiful baby in it.

Prosperity and the love of sweet children await you.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Rocking your own baby in a cradle — worry about a family member's health; be more attentive to them.
  • For a young woman rocking a cradle — she should beware of gossip.
