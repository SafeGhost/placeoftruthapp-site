A lizard in a dream

You see a lizard.

Enemies will try to attack you.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Killing a lizard means you will win back a lost reputation or money.
  • If it gets away, you will face annoying setbacks in love and work.
  • For a woman scratched by a lizard, the dream foretells a hard stretch: arm yourself with patience.
