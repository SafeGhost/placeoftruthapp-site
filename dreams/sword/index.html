A sword in a dream

You wear a sword.

You will hold a visible position with honor.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Having your sword taken means losing to a rival.
  • Others carrying swords mean quarrels may turn dangerous.
  • A broken sword means despair, so don't give in to it.
