Reading in a dream

You are reading.

You will excel at a task that seems difficult.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Seeing others read — your friends will be kind and well disposed toward you.
  • Discussing what you read — your gift with words will grow.
  • Blurry text that makes no sense — worries and disappointments.
