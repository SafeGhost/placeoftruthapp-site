A wedding ring in a dream

You see your wedding ring.

If the ring shines, worries and infidelity will pass you by.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • A lost or broken ring points to sadness and a chill in the relationship.
  • A wedding ring on someone else's hand warns you not to take your promises lightly.
