A chandelier in a dream

You see a chandelier.

Unexpected success will let you enjoy comfort and even luxury.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • A broken or dirty chandelier — a bad deal will hurt your finances.
  • Its light goes out — look after your health and don't celebrate your plans too early.
