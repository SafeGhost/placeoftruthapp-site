The sea in a dream

You see the sea.

On the surface all will be well, but inside you will long for something greater.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Hearing the lonely sound of the sea: a lonely spell; reach out to people close to you.
  • For a young woman, gliding over the sea with her partner: her dearest hopes come true and a happy union.
