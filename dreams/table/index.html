A table in a dream

You set the table.

Happy gatherings and well-being await you.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • An empty table means money trouble or disagreements.
  • A broken table means declining fortune.
  • A table moving by itself means you'll long for change.
  • A dirty tablecloth means disobedient children and quarrels after a celebration.
