Shoes in a dream

You put on new shoes.

Changes will work in your favor.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Polishing shoes: your affairs will improve, with good reason to rejoice.
  • Shabby, dirty shoes: harsh criticism will make you enemies.
  • Shoes that pinch: someone will make fun of you.
  • Untied laces: quarrels and losses.
  • Losing your shoes: a breakup.
