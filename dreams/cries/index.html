Cries in a dream

You hear cries for help.

Serious trouble lies ahead, but if you stay alert you will get through it and even come out ahead.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • A cry of surprise — help will come from where you least expect it.
  • The roar of wild animals — stay alert and take no needless risks.
  • Relatives or friends calling for help — they are struggling right now; support them.
