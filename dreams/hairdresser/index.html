A hairdresser in a dream

You are sitting at the hairdresser's.

You may get dragged into a noisy story because of someone else's indiscretion.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • For a woman, having her hair dyed: ill-wishers will try to hurt her reputation, but she'll stand firm.
  • Having her hair styled: don't chase trifles.
