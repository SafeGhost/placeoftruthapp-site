Jealousy in a dream

You feel jealous.

Ill-wishers and petty people are around you. Do not let them sow discord.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Being jealous over a loved one: you will want to push a rival aside.
  • A young woman jealous over her boyfriend: she may feel he prefers someone else.
  • Being jealous over work matters: everyday hassles.
