Bread in a dream

You share bread with others.

A secure living for life.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Good fresh bread within reach is a kind sign.
  • Lots of stale or spoiled bread means hard times; be thrifty.
  • For a woman, eating bread means her children's stubbornness will cause her a lot of fuss.
