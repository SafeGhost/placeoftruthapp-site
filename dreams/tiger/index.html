A tiger in a dream

A tiger comes toward you.

Enemies will pester you. Don't show weakness.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Fighting off or killing a tiger means great success in everything.
  • A tiger running away means you'll overcome resistance and rise high.
  • A caged tiger means you'll outwit rivals.
  • A tiger skin rug means luxury and ease.
