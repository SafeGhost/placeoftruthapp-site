Fruit in a dream

You see fruit ripening among the leaves.

Ripening fruit points to a prosperous future.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Green, unripe fruit: hasty decisions and wasted effort.
  • Buying or selling fruit: lots of work but little income.
  • Eating ripe fruit: changeable luck.
