A cross in a dream

You see a cross.

Trouble lies ahead — put your affairs in order in advance.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Someone carrying a cross — you will be asked to help with a good cause.
  • A crucifix — trouble that will touch others besides you.
  • Kissing a crucifix — you will accept a trial calmly.
  • For a young woman wearing a crucifix — modesty and kindness will bring her love and good fortune.
