Soup in a dream

You are eating soup.

Good news and comfort await you.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Watching others eat soup: promising acquaintances and a chance of marriage.
  • For a young woman, making soup: a comfortable life.
  • Eating creamy seafood soup: a quarrel followed by making up.
