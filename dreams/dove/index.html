A dove in a dream

You see a flock of white doves.

Peaceful joys and happy changes ahead.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Doves building a nest mean peace and harmony at home.
  • A dove bringing a letter means good news from distant friends and lovers making up.
  • The mournful cooing of a dove brings sadness and disappointment.
