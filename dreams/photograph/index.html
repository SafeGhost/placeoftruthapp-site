A photograph in a dream

You look at photographs.

Beware: deception is close.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Receiving a photo of your beloved — they are less loyal than they try to seem.
  • Keeping other people’s photos while in a relationship — awkward secrets may come out.
  • Having your own photo taken — carelessness will cause trouble for you and others.
