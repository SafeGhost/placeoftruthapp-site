A rival in a dream

You have a rival.

You are too slow to stand up for yourself and may lose favor with important people.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • A rival gets the better of you: you have grown too comfortable and let things slide.
  • You beat your rival: a step up and a good choice of partner.
  • For a young woman, the dream says to cherish the love she already has.
