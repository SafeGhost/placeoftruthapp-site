A gun in a dream

You hear a gunshot.

An unsettling dream: hold on to your job and manage your affairs more carefully.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Shooting someone: an act you'll be ashamed of.
  • Being shot: ill-wishers will pester you, so look after your health.
  • For a woman, shooting in a dream means quarrels and gossip.
