Clothes in a dream

You see clean new clothes.

Clean new clothes promise prosperity and well-being.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Dirty and torn clothes — someone is trying to deceive you; don't trust strangers.
  • A huge pile of clothes — don't overspend, or you'll run short of what you really need.
