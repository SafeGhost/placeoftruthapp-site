Perfume in a dream

You breathe in perfume.

Happy events are on their way.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Putting on perfume — you will seek admiration and get it.
  • A scent so strong it makes you dizzy — do not lose your head in joy.
  • Spilling perfume — you will lose something that gives you pleasure.
  • Breaking a bottle — a cherished wish may fall apart at the last moment.
  • Perfume as a gift — exciting but risky pleasures.
