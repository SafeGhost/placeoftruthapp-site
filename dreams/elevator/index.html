An elevator in a dream

You are going up in an elevator.

You will quickly rise to a good position and wealth.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Going down in an elevator means setbacks that will knock you off balance.
  • If the elevator leaves without you, you will narrowly avoid a disappointment.
  • A stopped elevator warns of danger.
