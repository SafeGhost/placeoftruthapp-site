Electricity in a dream

You dream of electricity.

Sudden changes will happen around you, but they will bring neither gain nor joy.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Getting an electric shock warns of danger.
  • A live wire means enemies will disrupt your plans.
  • If you travel instantly along a wire, you will overcome obstacles and turn others' plans to your advantage.
