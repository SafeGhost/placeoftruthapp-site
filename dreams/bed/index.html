A bed in a dream

You see a bed.

A clean white bed means peace: your worries will soon ease.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Making a bed brings a new admirer and pleasant work.
  • Lying in bed in a strange room means unexpected visitors.
  • Sleeping on a bed in the open air promises delightful experiences and a chance to improve your fortunes.
  • Wetting the bed points to troubles that will break your daily routine.
