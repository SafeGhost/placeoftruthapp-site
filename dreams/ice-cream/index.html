An ice cream in a dream

You are eating ice cream.

The things you have started will end well.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Children eating ice cream: prosperity and happiness.
  • Sour ice cream: an unexpected snag will spoil your fun.
  • Melted ice cream: the joy you expect will stall.
