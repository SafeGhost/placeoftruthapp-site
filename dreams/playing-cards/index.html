Playing cards in a dream

You are playing cards with friends for fun.

Long-held hopes will come true, and small troubles will go away.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Playing for money means serious difficulties.
  • Losing means meeting enemies.
  • Winning means you will prove yourself right, though not easily.
  • Diamonds mean wealth, hearts mean loyalty and a cosy home, clubs mean a demanding partner.
