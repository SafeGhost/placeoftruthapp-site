A needle in a dream

You sew with a needle.

Others' indifference may hurt you, so don't expect sympathy where there is none.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Threading a needle means you will have to care for people outside your family.
  • Looking for a needle foretells needless worry.
  • Finding one means you will have friends who value you.
  • Breaking one warns of loneliness and short money.
