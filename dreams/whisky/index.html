Whisky in a dream

You see bottles of whisky.

You will watch over your affairs carefully and make them grow.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Drinking whisky alone warns you not to put your own interests above your friends.
  • Whisky seldom promises good: you will get what you want only after several disappointments.
