A hospital in a dream

You are a patient in a hospital.

There may be fuss and worry around you, but it will pass you by. Take care of yourself.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Visiting patients in hospital: worrying news from people far away.
  • Leaving the hospital: you will slip away from cunning rivals.
