Christ in a dream

You see Christ.

Many peaceful days lie ahead, full of plenty, knowledge and joy.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • The Christ child with the wise men — peace and contentment.
  • Christ in the garden of Gethsemane — longing for loved ones far away and a hunger for change.
  • Christ driving the traders from the temple — honest work will defeat ill-wishers.
