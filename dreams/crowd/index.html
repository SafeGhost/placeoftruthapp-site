A crowd in a dream

You see a well-dressed crowd at a party.

Pleasant time with friends awaits you.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • The party is spoiled — disappointment and a cooling friendship.
  • A crowd in the street — a busy, successful period.
  • Trying to be heard over a crowd — you will push your interests forward.
  • Many people in dark clothes — small upsets.
