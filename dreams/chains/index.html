Chains in a dream

You are bound in chains.

An unfair burden is about to be placed on your shoulders.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Breaking the chains means you will free yourself from an unpleasant task or commitment.
  • Seeing chains means slander and envy.
  • Others in chains face hard times.
