A hurricane in a dream

A hurricane is heading toward you.

A tense time is ahead: you will have to fight hard to keep your affairs on track.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • A hurricane tears a house apart while you pull someone from the wreckage: big changes and moves, though peace will not come at once.
  • Looking at the damage after a hurricane: trouble will pass close by but miss you.
