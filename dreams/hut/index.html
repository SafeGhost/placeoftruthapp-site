A hut in a dream

You see a hut.

Success will be modest, without big leaps.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Sleeping in a hut: dissatisfaction and tiredness, so look after your health.
  • A hut in a green meadow: prosperity, though happiness will come and go.
