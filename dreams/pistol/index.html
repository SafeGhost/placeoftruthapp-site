A pistol in a dream

You see a pistol.

Generally a sign of bad luck; be careful.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Hearing a shot — you will learn of a scheme against your interests.
  • Firing it yourself — do not let envy and resentment push you to take revenge on an innocent person.
