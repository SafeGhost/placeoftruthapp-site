A school in a dream

You are at school.

Success awaits you in study, creative work or writing.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Being a child at school again: hard times will make you long for the simple joys of the past.
  • Teaching a class: you strive for knowledge, but must first take care of daily needs.
  • Visiting your old school: present worries weigh on you; give yourself a rest.
