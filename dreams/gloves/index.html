Gloves in a dream

You put on new gloves.

You'll be careful and thrifty but not stingy; disputes at work will be settled in your favour.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Old or torn gloves: deceit and losses.
  • Losing your gloves: you'll have to rely on yourself alone.
  • Finding a pair of gloves: a new romance.
