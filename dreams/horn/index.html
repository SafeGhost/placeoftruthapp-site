A horn in a dream

You hear the sound of a horn.

Happy news will arrive soon.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • A broken horn: an unpleasant surprise, so be careful.
  • Children playing with horns: harmony at home.
  • A woman blowing a horn: she wants to marry more than her partner does.
