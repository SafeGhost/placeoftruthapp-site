A fence in a dream

You climb a fence.

Reaching the top means your efforts will be crowned with success.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Falling off a fence: you have taken on something beyond your strength.
  • Squeezing through a fence: questionable ways to your goal.
  • Building a fence: thrift and hard work lay the ground for future wealth.
