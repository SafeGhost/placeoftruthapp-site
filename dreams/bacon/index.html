Bacon in a dream

You eat bacon with someone.

A good sign if the company is pleasant and everything is clean: comfort and luck.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Rancid bacon — annoying setbacks and careless mistakes.
  • Curing bacon cleanly — good; in smoke and salt — trouble.
