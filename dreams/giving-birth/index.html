Giving birth in a dream

You are giving birth to a baby.

Great joy and an unexpected gain are coming.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • For a married woman, this dream promises happiness in the family.
  • For a single woman, it says be careful in new relationships and do not trust too quickly.
