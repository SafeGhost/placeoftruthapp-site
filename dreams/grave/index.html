A grave in a dream

You see a freshly dug grave.

The dream warns that you may suffer for other people's mistakes; be careful in your dealings.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Digging a grave and finishing it: you'll overcome your opponents.
  • If the sun is shining as well, good will come out of the trouble.
  • Looking into an empty grave: disappointment and friends drifting away.
