Birds in a dream

You see birds flying.

Prosperity is coming: everything unpleasant will give way to a wave of good change.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Birds with bright, beautiful feathers mean good luck, and for a woman a happy meeting with a reliable partner.
  • A wounded bird means sorrow over loved ones.
  • Catching a bird is not a bad sign.
  • Talking birds mean you lack clarity in an important matter.
