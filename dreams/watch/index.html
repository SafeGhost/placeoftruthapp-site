A watch in a dream

You see a wristwatch.

Well-planned investments will bring you income.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Checking the time means rivals may get in your way.
  • Breaking a watch points to worry and loss.
  • Losing one means quarrels at home.
  • Stealing one means a fierce opponent will appear.
  • Giving one as a gift warns against wasting energy on idle fun.
