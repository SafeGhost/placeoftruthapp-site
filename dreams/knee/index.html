A knee in a dream

Your knees ache and will not bend.

Some trouble may be ahead. Be more careful in your affairs and look after your health.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Knees that are too big: sudden bad luck.
  • A woman seeing she has pretty, smooth knees: many admirers, but serious intentions will take a while.
