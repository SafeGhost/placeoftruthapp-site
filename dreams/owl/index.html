An owl in a dream

You hear the muffled hoot of an owl.

Look after your health and don't take needless risks: the dream asks for caution.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Seeing an owl means someone is secretly speaking ill of you, so beware of rivals.
  • A dead owl promises a narrow escape from serious trouble.
  • The dream may bring worrying news about people far away.
