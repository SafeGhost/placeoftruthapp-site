A cat in a dream

You see a cat.

Bad luck, unless you chase it out of sight.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • A cat attacking you means enemies will try to blacken your name.
  • Driving it away means you will overcome big obstacles.
  • A thin, dirty cat means worrying news about a friend, but all will be well.
  • A meowing cat means a false friend is working against you behind your back.
  • A cat scratching you means someone will try to take the profit you earned.
  • A white cat means a tangle that only seems harmless.
