A forehead in a dream

You see a fine, smooth forehead.

People will value your good judgement and fairness.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • An ugly forehead: annoyances in private matters.
  • Stroking your child's forehead: friends will sincerely praise your children.
