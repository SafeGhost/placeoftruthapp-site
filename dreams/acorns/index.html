Acorns in a dream

You see acorns.

Pleasant things and good gains lie ahead.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Picking acorns from the ground — success after long work.
  • Shaking them from the oak — your wishes in work or love will come true fast.
  • Rotten acorns — disappointment.
  • Pulling green ones off — haste will hurt you.
