Coins in a dream

You see gold coins.

Gold coins mean great prosperity, travel and vivid impressions.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Silver coins — discord at home, but if they are clean, shiny and in your own hands — good luck.
  • Copper coins — hard work and low spirits.
  • For a young woman getting a silver coin from her sweetheart — a breakup.
