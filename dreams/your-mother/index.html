Your mother in a dream

You see your mother at home.

What you have started will bear good fruit.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Talking with your mother brings good news about what worries you.
  • Your mother calling you means you have taken a wrong turn, so review your affairs.
  • Your mother crying tells you to take care of yourself and visit her.
  • A sad or worn-out mother foretells sadness.
