Triplets in a dream

You see triplets.

Something you feared would fail will end in success.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • For a man, dreaming his wife has triplets means a long dispute will end well.
  • Newborn triplets crying mean quarrels that will be quickly settled.
  • For a young woman, having triplets means disappointment in love but also wealth.
