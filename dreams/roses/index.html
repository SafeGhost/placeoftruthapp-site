Roses in a dream

You see roses in full, fragrant bloom.

A joyful event is near, and the one you love will be faithful to you.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • For a young woman, picking roses means a proposal she will welcome.
  • Withered roses: missing loved ones who are far away.
  • Breathing in their scent: pure joy.
  • A rosebush without flowers: growing prosperity.
