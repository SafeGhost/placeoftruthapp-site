A farewell in a dream

You say goodbye to someone.

Not the kindest dream: you may hear unpleasant news about people who are far away.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • For a young woman, saying goodbye to her sweetheart hints that his feelings are cooling.
  • If she feels no sadness, others will soon comfort her.
