A handshake in a dream

You shake someone's hand.

You will be loved and respected for your kindness.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Shaking hands with an important person: honor and goodwill from strangers.
  • Having to reach up to do it: rivalry.
  • Someone's hands are dirty: there are enemies among your seeming friends.
