Wheat in a dream

You see fields of wheat.

Your affairs will gain good prospects.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Ripe wheat means a secure living and happy love.
  • Grain in sacks points to victory soon and lasting feelings.
  • Grain getting wet in the barn warns you to guard what you have earned from others.
  • Climbing a hill by holding on to the stalks means great success in anything you choose.
