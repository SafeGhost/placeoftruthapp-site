A chimney in a dream

You see a chimney.

Something unpleasant is about to happen, and worrying news about someone's health may arrive.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • A fire burning in the hearth — great good is coming.
  • A chimney covered in ivy — happiness after sadness.
  • A chimney collapsing — upsets in the family.
  • Climbing up a chimney — you will escape a trap set for you.
