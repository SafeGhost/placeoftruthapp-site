Lending in a dream

You lend someone money.

You may find it hard to pay your own bills.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • Lending things means generosity that may leave you short.
  • Refusing to lend means you will protect your interests and keep friends' respect.
  • Being offered a loan yourself means prosperity and close friendships.
