A raft in a dream

You see a raft.

You will move to a new place, and what you start there will succeed.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Floating on a raft — a journey with an unclear outcome; if you reach your goal, good fortune awaits.
  • The raft breaks apart — look after yourself and your friends on the road.
