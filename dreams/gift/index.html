A gift in a dream

Someone gives you a gift.

You'll keep up with all your payments and be lucky in money matters or love.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Giving a gift yourself: displeasure from others and setbacks; don't expect thanks.
  • For a young woman, rich gifts from her sweetheart promise a happy, comfortable marriage.
