A tunnel in a dream

You go through a tunnel.

A tricky time is starting in work and love. Be patient.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • A tunnel caving in means failure and spiteful enemies.
  • Looking into a tunnel means you'll soon face a hard decision.
