Lettuce in a dream

You see fresh green lettuce growing.

After a small embarrassment, you will get something you have long wanted.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Eating lettuce means petty jealousy or a small rift.
  • Picking it warns you not to let jealousy torment you.
  • For a woman sowing lettuce, the dream advises looking after her health.
  • Buying lettuce warns you not to work against yourself.
