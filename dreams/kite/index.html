A kite in a dream

You are flying a kite.

Success may look bigger than it really is. Check what your plans rest on.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • The kite crashes to the ground: disappointment.
  • Children flying kites: pleasant, easy activities.
  • The kite flies out of sight: do not build your hopes too high.
