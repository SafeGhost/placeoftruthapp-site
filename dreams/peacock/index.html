A peacock in a dream

You see a peacock.

Sorrows may hide behind glitter and luxury, so don't be dazzled by appearances.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • For a woman, owning peacocks warns she may misjudge someone's honesty.
  • Hearing a peacock's harsh cry as it spreads its tail means a beautiful, charming person will cause you unease.
