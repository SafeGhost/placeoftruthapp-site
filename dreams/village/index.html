A village in a dream

You find yourself in a village.

Good health and a secure living await you.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Returning to the village of your childhood brings pleasant surprises and good news from distant friends.
  • A run-down or blurry village points to sadness and trouble.
