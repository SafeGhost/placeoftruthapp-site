A monkey in a dream

You see a monkey.

Sly people will flatter you for their own gain, so don't trust sweet words.

Usually a warning

Other versions of this dream

  • A dead monkey means your worst rivals will soon back off.
  • Feeding a monkey warns of a flatterer who may betray you.
  • For a young woman, the dream advises not to delay in love so as not to stir jealousy.
