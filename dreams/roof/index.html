A roof in a dream

You are standing on a roof.

Great success awaits you.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Fearing you will fall: you will rise, but your place will be shaky.
  • A roof caving in: a sudden blow.
  • Fixing or building a roof: your income will grow fast.
  • Sleeping on a roof: you are safe from enemies and full of strength.
