A palace in a dream

You wander through a palace, admiring its splendor.

Your prospects are improving, and a new, higher standing awaits you.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Seeing elegant people dancing and talking in the palace promises profitable, pleasant acquaintances.
  • For a young woman, being among the guests as an equal foretells a better life through marriage or family help.
  • The dream advises relying on honest work rather than daydreams.
