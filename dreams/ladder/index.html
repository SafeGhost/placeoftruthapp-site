A ladder in a dream

You climb a ladder.

Success and real happiness are coming your way.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • A ladder set up for you to climb: your energy will bring you to a prominent place.
  • Falling off a ladder means gloom and failed deals.
  • A broken ladder means plans that fall through.
  • Climbing down means disappointment.
  • Escaping captivity by a ladder means success after a risky road.
  • Feeling dizzy on the way up warns you not to let a new position go to your head.
