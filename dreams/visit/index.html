A visit in a dream

You go visiting.

A pleasant occasion is coming soon.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • An unpleasant visit means spiteful people may spoil your fun.
  • A friend visiting you brings good news.
  • A sad, tired guest means small disappointments.
  • A pale guest dressed in black or white is a hint to mind your health and take care.
