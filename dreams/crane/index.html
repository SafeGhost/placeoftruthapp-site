A crane in a dream

You see a flock of cranes in the sky.

Cranes flying south — a happy reunion with people far away and faithfulness in love.

Usually a good sign

Other versions of this dream

  • Cranes flying north — things will go slowly, and a letdown is possible.
  • Cranes landing — something important is about to happen.
